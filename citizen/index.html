How GramSetu Operates

A direct, cloud-connected pipeline connecting rural citizens with their designated Panchayat Development Officers.

📸

1. Capture & Report

Snap a photo using your mobile camera, describe the issue, and submit from anywhere.

📍

2. Auto-Route by Region

The backend automatically routes your grievance strictly to your Gram Panchayat administration.

👷‍♂️

3. Official Inspection

Designated field officers review photographic evidence and dispatch maintenance crews.

✅

4. Verified Resolution

Receive real-time timeline updates and official officer remarks once the defect is repaired.

Supported Civic Services

Report any public defect across all essential village service domains.

🛣️

Roads

Potholes, broken asphalt, culvert slab collapses, and tractor route damage.

💡

Street Lights

Defective solar lamps, short-circuited pole wiring, and unlit village lanes.

🚰

Water Supply

Main pipe ruptures, dry borewells, handpump breakdowns, and contamination.

🧼

Sanitation

Community toilet hygiene, stagnant water spraying, and disinfection drives.

🌊

Drainage

Blocked open stormwater gutters, silt accumulation, and sewage backflow.

♻️

Waste Management

Overflowing garbage collection points and delayed refuse tipper collection.

⚡

Electricity

Hanging low-tension power cables and damaged agricultural feeder transformers.

🏫

Public Infrastructure

Primary health sub-center repairs, Anganwadi building maintenance, and community halls.

📋

Other

Any civic grievance that falls within the jurisdiction of the Gram Panchayat.

About GramSetu

Bridging Villages with Transparent Governance

GramSetu is an open digital platform developed to eliminate bureaucratic delays in rural grievance resolution. By combining mobile photography, GPS validation, and regional database isolation, citizens directly empower Panchayat Development Officers with verified on-ground intelligence.
